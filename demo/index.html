Which station

Direct talks to the station on the cable. Remote relays through it to a second station placed on its coupling stick, so you can configure that one without unplugging anything. Commands go to whichever is selected — including the destructive ones.

A sleeping station takes a while to rouse — 11 and 23 seconds on the two measured here — so give it up to half a minute.
